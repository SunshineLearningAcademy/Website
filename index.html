Who We Are

About Sunshine Academy

Founded on the belief that education changes lives, Sunshine Academy is a community-powered nonprofit dedicated to giving students the tools, confidence, and support they need to reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide free, high-quality tutoring and mentorship that empowers every student to succeed academically and beyond.

Our Vision

A community where every child — regardless of circumstance — has access to the educational support they deserve.

Our Values

Compassion, equity, patience, and perseverance guide everything we do, from one-on-one sessions to group workshops.
